## 🛠️ Step-by-Step AI Video Creation Workflow

1. **Voiceover Generation**:
   * Copy the VO scripts above into **ElevenLabs**.
   * Pick a refined, warm female voice (e.g., *Rachel*, *Sarah*, or a custom trained calm voice).
   * Generate and export the MP3 files.
2. **Video Clip Generation**:
   * Paste the visual prompts into **Kling AI (v1.5 / v2.0)** or **Runway Gen-3**.
   * Select **16:9** for YouTube/landscape or **9:16 / 3:4** for Instagram Reels & TikTok.
   * Generate 5-second video clips for each scene.
3. **Editing & Assembly** *(CapCut or Premiere Pro)*:
   * Import the generated AI video clips and your ElevenLabs voiceover.
   * Add high-contrast text animations matching your brand font (e.g., *Playfair Display* / *Plus Jakarta Sans*).
   * Layer subtle background music at **-22dB** under the voiceover.
   * Add your Selar checkout link / Call-To-Action end-card.
