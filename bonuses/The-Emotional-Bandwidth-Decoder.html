The 3-Second Timing Test

Before initiating an important conversation or request, ask yourself:

  • Is his attention focused and calm, or is he multitasking/stressed?
  • Am I speaking out of quiet self-certainty, or anxious urgency?
  • Can this request be stated in one sentence without a defensive justification?
